# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friend groups splitting shared expenses — roommates, trips, dinners, recurring bills. Casual social context, quick mobile-first interactions, often between people who trust each other but want clarity.

## Product Purpose

Split expenses with friends. No spreadsheets needed. Track shared costs, split bills evenly or custom, and settle up — all in one warm, delightful place.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, delightful.

## Anti-references

Cold/corporate finance apps — no bank app vibes, no Mint-style dashboards, no "serious money" UI. Avoid anything that feels like accounting software or a spreadsheet.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth without clutter** — the palette (burnt terracotta, warm earth tones) should feel welcoming, not aggressive. Space breathes.
2. **Trust through clarity** — every balance, split, and settlement must be immediately understandable at a glance.
3. **Delight in the mundane** — expense tracking should feel pleasant, not like a chore. Micro-copy, subtle motion, considered spacing.
4. **Mobile-first social** — built for quick taps between friends, not spreadsheet sessions. Optimize for the "split a bill at dinner" moment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA (contrast ≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large text; keyboard navigable; screen reader support).
- Support prefers-reduced-motion.
